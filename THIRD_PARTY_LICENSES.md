# Third-party components and data

This repository contains integration code, metadata, and the approved voice-reference assets under `assets/triangle-strategy/`. Generated output remains excluded from the public repository. Third-party audio keeps the license and attribution requirements of its source; the repository MIT license does not replace those terms.

## OmniVoice Thai model

- Model: `hotdogs/omnivoice-thai`
- Source: Hugging Face
- License reported by the model card: Apache-2.0
- This repository does not redistribute the model weights.

## THAI-SER dataset

- Dataset: `airesearch/thai-ser`
- Source: Hugging Face
- License reported by the dataset card: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- Locally prepared reference clips derived from THAI-SER may be included under `assets/triangle-strategy/`; those files remain subject to the applicable CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution/share-alike obligations.

## Other voice-reference sources

`hf_voice_reference_manifest.csv` records provenance and license metadata collected during local voice-reference research. Some candidate sources are marked CC BY 4.0 and some CC BY-NC 4.0. Approved files under `assets/triangle-strategy/` may be part of the public release; their individual source/license terms still apply, including non-commercial restrictions where applicable.

Before redistributing any third-party audio or derived voice pack, review the source license and attribution requirements for that specific asset.

## Game-specific content

Game dialogue CSVs and generated chapter audio are intentionally excluded from the public repository. Approved voice-reference assets under `assets/triangle-strategy/` are included separately from generated game audio and remain governed by their source licenses/provenance.
